  3. Prompts that aren't recorded are read out by a computer voice in English, so record all the Hausa ones before the demo.

Keep them safe

On Render's free plan, recordings are wiped when the app redeploys. When you're happy with them, download all recordings, unzip into the project and push to GitHub, so they're part of the code.
